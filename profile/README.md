# FRAGMENTS LABS

> **An independent software lab.**

Fragments is an independent software lab focused on building tools that solve real problems.

We don't believe in tools that try to do everything. We believe in focused, well-built software that people actually want to use.

---

## Built to solve. Built with purpose.

Every product starts with a problem worth solving.

Fragments didn't begin as an ecosystem. It began with a series of problems worth solving. Over time, those solutions became products. Together, they became Fragments.

---

## Products

### Orbit

**Your development workspace, powered by agents.**

Orbit brings development, agents, and tools together in a single space. Each product can work on its own; connected to Orbit, it becomes part of a larger workflow.

🌐 https://orbit.fragmentslab.com

---

### Nodara

**Your device, part of your development flow.**

Nodara connects your phone to your development environment through a simple QR code, letting you mirror, manage, and control the device. Integrated with Orbit or any agent through MCP, it turns real devices into part of development, testing, and documentation.

🌐 https://nodaraapp.com

---

### Fracta

**Everything you need to work with your data.**

Fracta brings everything you need to work with your databases into a modern interface. Connect multiple MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQLite databases, work across tabs, smart filters, query builders, imports, exports, and backups — with support for agent-driven workflows and deep integration with Orbit.

🚧 Currently in development.
